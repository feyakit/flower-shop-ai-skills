# Photo Optimization Playbook

## Common Flower Photo Problems

- Dim exposure: flowers look old or grey.
- Dirty whites: wrapping paper and background look yellow/green.
- Clipped highlights: white roses or paper become flat blocks.
- Oversaturated reds/yellows: petals lose texture.
- Low local contrast: bouquet lacks depth.
- Busy edge clutter: background competes with the bouquet.
- Bad crop: bouquet is too low, too centered, or has no cover-text space.

## Preset Guidance

**natural**

- Use for phone photos that already look close to reality.
- Gentle brightness, contrast, color, and sharpening.
- Best default for customer-facing product posts.

**high-key**

- Use for white wrapping, cream/pink bouquets, bright desktop product photos.
- Slightly brighter and cleaner.
- Keep highlights protected; do not flatten white flowers.

**warm**

- Use for indoor shop photos, wood tables, warm lights, cozy scenes.
- Adds a tiny warm lift while controlling yellow cast.

## Xiaohongshu Export Sizes

- 3:4: `1080x1440`, strong default for flower-shop posts.
- 4:5: `1080x1350`, good for product close-ups and cover-first posts.
- Upload copy: `720x960` or `900x1200` when browser uploads time out.

## Image-Edit Prompt Pattern

When using image generation/editing, preserve the product identity:

```text
Keep the same bouquet, flower colors, wrapping style, camera angle, and realistic petal texture.
Make the image brighter and cleaner for a Xiaohongshu flower-shop post.
Reduce background clutter, preserve natural shadows, avoid plastic-looking flowers,
and keep the bouquet commercially realistic.
```

Only change bouquet structure, wrapping, flower species, or background when the user explicitly asks.
