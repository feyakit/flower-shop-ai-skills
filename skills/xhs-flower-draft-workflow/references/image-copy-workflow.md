# Image And Copy Workflow

## Image Optimization

1. Select cover image by sellability:
   - Human-held bouquet/flower basket: best for size, gifting impact, and first-click appeal.
   - Product-only clean image: best for detail support.
2. Export:
   - Master: `1080x1440`, 3:4 vertical.
   - Upload copy: `900x1200` or `720x960` if browser upload is unstable.
3. Natural edits:
   - Lift exposure and flower midtones.
   - Reduce yellow/gray cast in whites and wall.
   - Preserve petal veins and real texture.
   - Avoid neon saturation, fake blur, invented flowers, or changing bouquet structure.

## Title Pattern

Use a Xiaohongshu-native hook plus local keyword. Keep it short.

Good patterns:

- `{产品情绪卖点}｜{城市}花店`
- `{人群词}会心动的{产品词}｜{城市}花店`
- `{产品词}太会哄{人群词}｜{城市}花店`
- `{场景词}送这束很稳｜{城市}花店`

Avoid flat directory titles like `{城市}花店｜粉白花篮适合送礼` unless the user explicitly asks for SEO-only titles.

## Body Shape

```text
{情绪开场：收到/送出时的感受}
{1-2 行让买家想象场景}

{花材、色系、容器/包装、体量、风格}
{为什么适合送礼：出片、仪式感、不踩雷、有尺寸感}

适合送{人群}
{场景列表}

📍{城市}花店
🚗{城市}同城可配送
💌可搭配手写贺卡，支持按预算、色系和送花对象定制
```

Do not invent price, availability, delivery time, or exact store location.
